ShowCue User Manual

Tap a pad: starts its track. If TV Display is ON and a video is attached, the video starts with it.

Hold a pad for 3 seconds: choose an imported audio track and optional video from the ShowCue library.

Drag a pad: reorder cues. Maximum 50 pads per setlist.

Lyrics: open Lyrics to follow the selected song. Lyrics are local to the operator screen and are not sent to TV.

Menu: load/export playlists and import files.

Settings: choose 8/12/20/50 visible pads, volume sync, light/dark display, finish behaviour and pad size.

TV Display: ON means attached cue video is shown. OFF keeps video hidden while audio continues.

Audio: iPhone, Android and PC file import is supported through the device file picker. Actual playback formats depend on the browser/device codec.
